# LinkedIn blurb

I open-sourced a small project that sits at the intersection of my two worlds: insurance and cloud engineering. It is an educational life insurance illustration engine, a Python/FastAPI service that projects a generic flexible-premium policy year by year under guaranteed, midpoint and current assumptions, with the real mechanics written out in readable code: premium loads, monthly cost of insurance on the net amount at risk, fixed and indexed crediting with caps and floors, the death-benefit corridor, surrender charges and lapse. Around it is the platform I would want for any production service: a non-root container on ECS Fargate behind an ALB, everything in Terraform, GitHub Actions deploying through OIDC with no stored cloud keys, mypy strict and 100% test coverage, Bandit, Trivy and CodeQL scans, Terraform plans posted to pull requests, and CloudWatch dashboards, alarms and structured logs. All assumptions are synthetic and it is not connected to any real product; the goal was to show how insurance domain logic and solid DevOps practice fit together. Repo: https://github.com/femi-alla/illustration-engine
